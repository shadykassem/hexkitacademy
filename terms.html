Terms of Enrollment

Eligibility

Workshops are for students ages 12-18. Enrollment is completed by a parent or legal guardian.

What's included

Per the What's included list on the pricing page for your session.

Payment

Seats are $339 early bird / $449 regular (USD) unless otherwise published. A second sibling is $50 off via SIBLING50 on the Stripe page. Payment links are emailed after your signed waiver is confirmed. Your seat is reserved when payment completes.

Conduct and safety

Students follow instructor directions and cell safety rules at all times. We may remove a participant immediately for safety violations or conduct endangering anyone. Tuition for that session is then non-refundable (see Refunds).

Cancellations and refunds

Governed by our Refund policy.

Photos and media

Photo/media use is a separate opt-in on the waiver, never bundled with enrollment. We never publish a student's full name without separate written permission.

Student work

Your teen keeps their program files and demo video. We may use anonymized, non-identifying photos of equipment or processes.

Liability

Participation is governed by the signed Workshop Participation Agreement and Liability Waiver (California law; venue in Orange County, California).

Changes

We may update these terms. The version in effect when you enroll applies to your session.
