Privacy Policy

Effective: September 24, 2026

Who we are

Hexkit Academy (hexkitacademy.com), Orange County / Irvine, California. Contact: waitlist@hexkitacademy.com.

What we collect

From the waitlist form: parent/guardian name, email, phone; student's first name only and age band; optional note. If you enroll: the signed workshop waiver (participant and parent details, emergency contacts, medical notes you choose to share, photo/media preference).

How we use it

To respond to waitlist signups, run enrollment and sessions, keep students safe (emergency contacts), and communicate about workshops. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their marketing.

Processors

Form submissions are delivered via Formspree. Online payments are processed by Stripe. Card details go directly to Stripe and we never see or store them.

Children's privacy

Our forms are completed by a parent or legal guardian, and workshops are for students ages 12-18. We collect only a student's first name and age band unless you enroll, in which case the waiver collects what safety requires.

Your rights (California)

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing waitlist@hexkitacademy.com. We will respond within 45 days.

Retention

We keep waitlist and enrollment records only as long as needed for the purposes above, then delete them on request.

Analytics

We do not currently use third-party analytics or advertising cookies on this site.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
