FAQ

Quick answers

Who is HEXKIT ACADEMY for?

Middle and high school students who want hands-on experience with real collaborative robots, not just games or toy kits.

Do parents need a tech background?

No. Sessions are supervised and structured. Parents get clear info when workshops open; you don’t need to be an engineer to enroll your student.

Is prior robotics or coding experience required?

No. Curiosity and willingness to work with tools matter more. We’ll meet students where they are.

Where are sessions held?

Orange County / Irvine area, hosted with partners including Urban Workshop, theCoderSchool, and Ardent. Exact locations announced with each session.

How much does it cost?

Course value is framed at $990. Early bird is $339, regular is $449. Second sibling gets $50 off. Payment links are emailed only after your signed waiver is confirmed.

How do I sign up?

Join the waitlist below. We’ll email when weekend workshops open. No payment required to join the list. For a paid seat, complete the waiver first.

How is this different from coding-only camps?

We emphasize a real Fairino 6-axis cobot, teach pendant time for every teen, repair and maintenance, plus a personal program, demo video, and portfolio certificate. Not screen-only coding tracks.
