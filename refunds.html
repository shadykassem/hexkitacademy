Refund and Cancellation Policy

  • Cancel 14 or more days before your session: full refund.
  • Cancel 7-13 days before: 50% refund, or free transfer to a later session.
  • Cancel fewer than 7 days before: the seat is non-refundable, but transfers once to a later session free of charge.
  • If we cancel a session for any reason: full refund, including any fees.
  • No-shows without notice are non-refundable.

To request a refund, email waitlist@hexkitacademy.com with subject "Refund - [student first name] - [session date]". Refunds go to the original payment method within 10 business days.
