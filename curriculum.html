What students learn

Every HEXKIT ACADEMY workshop follows our Industrial Cobot Intro course: the modules below plus a capstone reliability run, taught hands-on with a real Fairino 6-axis cobot. Safety first, real machine time from the start, no laptop-only drills.

  1. Robot history. Where industrial robots came from, and why cobots changed the shop floor.
  2. What is a cobot? What makes a collaborative robot different from a traditional arm.
  3. Compare and ROI. How shops decide a cobot is worth it: the real math behind automation.
  4. Safety gate and drills. The safety rules and drills every student passes before touching the arm, including the three-ways-to-stop drill.
  5. Cell and procedure. The startup, shutdown, and home procedure card, and how a real robot cell is organized.
  6. How the robot thinks. Coordinate frames, singularities, and hardstops, in plain language.
  7. First moves, part 1. Jogging the arm on the teach pendant.
  8. First moves, part 2. Planning and running deliberate moves.
  9. Programming labs. Writing, simulating, and testing real programs.
  10. Tooling and I/O. Grippers, tooling, and the I/O handshake project.
  11. Maintenance. The backup habit, inspection, and the daily pre-flight check.
  12. Troubleshooting. Diagnosing faults the way a technician does.
  13. Capstone: The Cell Challenge. An unattended reliability run, scored on fault-free operation.

What they take home

Their program files, a demo video of it running, and a Cobot Operator Foundations certificate of completion. The certificate documents completed coursework. It is not an accredited credential.
