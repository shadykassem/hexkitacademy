What students do

Real machines. Real skills.

High-level focus, no canned module list. Students work with industrial collaborative robots in a supervised workshop setting. Scrub the steps. This is the same four-part focus as the live page.

Program

Learn how cobots are programmed and tasked for real workshop jobs: motion, tooling, and workflow basics.

Repair and maintain

Hands-on hardware: inspect, diagnose, and care for real equipment the way a shop floor expects.

Safety

Industrial safety habits first: guards, procedures, and respect for machines that can move force.

Build judgment

More than coding drills: students leave with workshop confidence and a clearer path into STEM trades.
